"I grew up in Cartagena. The people who will cater your wedding, shoot your photos, and sail your guests to the ceremony are people I've known for years — not vendors I found online."

Marry Me in Cartagena is a full-service destination wedding company built around one idea: you shouldn't have to coordinate a wedding in a foreign country, in a language you don't speak, with vendors you've never met.

I handle venue selection, event design, catering, photography, legal paperwork, and guest logistics remotely, then join you on the ground for a confirmation visit before your wedding — and stay through every moment of the day itself.

Where your wedding could be

Playa Blanca

White sand and turquoise water, half an hour from the city — the classic Cartagena beach ceremony.

Isla del Encanto

A private island feel for couples who want their wedding genuinely secluded, just offshore.

Centro Histórico

Colonial balconies, bougainvillea, and four-hundred-year-old stone walls, right in the heart of the walled city.
